# 50 Prompts to Try With Your Own Binance Agent OS Agent

### Basic Market & Account Prompts (1–15)

1. Show my current USDT balance in the Agentic sub-account.
2. What is the current price and 24h change of BNB, BTC, and ETH?
3. Show me the live order book depth for BNBUSDT.
4. Give me a quick market summary for BNB right now.
5. Compare the 24h volume of BNB, SOL, and XRP.
6. What is the current bid-ask spread for BTCUSDT?
7. Show top 5 coins by 24h price change on Binance.
8. Is liquidity good for a $20 buy on BNB right now?
9. Show my full Agentic sub-account balances.
10. What was the highest and lowest price of BNB in the last 24 hours?
11. Give me funding rate for BTCUSDT perpetual (if available).
12. Show recent large buy and sell walls on ETHUSDT.
13. Summarize market sentiment using current order book data.
14. Check if BNB is trading near support or resistance based on order book.
15. Show me the current market status of the top 3 gainers.

### Content Creation Prompts (16–30)

16. Write a 60-second TikTok script about current BNB price action using live data.
17. Create a short X post explaining today’s BTC movement with real numbers.
18. Turn the current order book of ETH into a simple educational carousel idea.
19. Write a hook + 3 key points for a YouTube Short about BNB.
20. Create a “Market Update” script for my Instagram Reels using live data.
21. Write a beginner-friendly explanation of why BNB is moving today.
22. Generate 5 tweet ideas based on current market data.
23. Create a voice-over script for a 45-second market recap.
24. Write a comparison post: BNB vs ETH today.
25. Make a “What I’m watching this week” script using live data.
26. Turn current market data into a motivational trading mindset post.
27. Write a thread outline about order book reading for beginners.
28. Create a script that ends with a clear call-to-action for my audience.
29. Generate a poll question based on current BNB price action.
30. Write a honest “small trade I just made” style script.

### Trading & Execution Prompts (31–40)

31. Prepare a market buy of exactly $10 worth of BNB and wait for my approval.
32. Prepare a $15 buy of BTC and show full order details.
33. Suggest a simple $20 split between BNB and ETH.
34. Check liquidity and then prepare a $10 BNB buy if spread is good.
35. Show me what $25 of SOL would look like as a market order.
36. Prepare a trade but only if the spread is under 0.15%.
37. Create a micro-rebalance plan using $30 total.
38. Prepare a buy and also write a short content script about why I’m taking it.
39. Show risk of a $20 position on BNB at current volatility.
40. Prepare three different small trade options ($10, $15, $20) and let me choose.

### Advanced / Creative Prompts (41–50)

41. Act as my daily co-pilot: give market briefing + content idea + balance check.
42. Analyze BNB order book and tell me if a breakout or rejection is more likely.
43. Create a full 3-part content series idea based on today’s market.
44. Run a “safe mode” analysis: only suggest trades with high liquidity.
45. Combine market data + my balance and suggest the smartest small move today.
46. Pretend you are teaching my students: explain the current order book simply.
47. Create a weekly watchlist of 5 coins using live data.
48. Generate a risk warning based on current market conditions.
49. Do a full pipeline: data → insight → content script → micro-trade proposal.
50. End-of-day routine: summarize market + my account + one lesson for my audience.
